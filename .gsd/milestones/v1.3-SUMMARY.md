## Lessons Learned
- **Revit 2026 Tolerance**: Strictly enforces a minimum curve length (~0.00256'). 2D flattening can cause segments to become too small even if they were valid in 3D CAD. Defensive `try-catch` and larger `0.005` tolerances are necessary for stability.
- **Family Editor Transactions**: `LoadFamily` MUST be wrapped in a transaction in the *project* document, even if the family modification already committed its own transaction.
- **Category Retrieval**: Casting ElementId values to BuiltInCategory is reliable for built-in categories, but name-based fallback is essential for robustness across different project environments.
